This post contains the presentations' slides I gave during my master's degree. I started the master's program with a defined project: to study sound production in birds using physics and computer science. I chose IME-USP because of the [Computer Music Group](https://compmus.ime.usp.br/en/), a research group with extensive experience in analyzing sound and a strong multidisciplinary focus, guided by Professor [Marcelo Gomes de Queiroz](https://www.ime.usp.br/mqz/), an expert in both music and computer science. The master's project is developed with the guidance of [Gabriel Mindlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_B._Mindlin), from the [LSD](http://www.lsd.df.uba.ar/) research gropu at the University of Buenos Aires (UBA), as coadvisor, a prominent figure in the area of sound production in birds.
